Data Driven NYC: 15 Years of Predictions

I took transcripts from 518 Data Driven NYC and MAD Podcast talks, from 2011 to 2026, and tracked two things: how the language of the industry changed, and which of the predictions speakers made actually came true.

Of 554 predictions, 208 could be scored so far. Predictions about infrastructure held up much better than predictions about applications: 58% correct versus 33%.
